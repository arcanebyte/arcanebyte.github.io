The Apple IIe Card goes in any Lisa expansion slot. A real 65C02 runs the code, and the
Lisa supplies the screen, keyboard, ports, clock, and disks. The Apple's speaker plays
through the Lisa's, with a volume dial on the card.

Disks are image files on an SD card on the card itself. The card emulates a Disk II with
two drives and a ProDOS volume, plus a parallel printer card, a Super Serial Card on Lisa
port A, an 80-column card, and a ThunderClock-compatible clock.
