Retro is cosmo! The ArcaneByte WiFiModem is a Hayes-compatible wifi modem for vintage
computers with an RS232 serial port that can be used to connect to Internet-based
resources.

Now you can connect to your favorite BBS using an IP address rather than a phone
number. Or, you can transfer files using zmodem or Kermit @ 1200 baud for that true
vintage experience. If you want to host a BBS, enable the TCP/IP listener function to
'dial in' to your retro machine. The possibilities really are endless!

The ArcaneByte WiFiModem is a hand-built device based on the ESP8266 chipset and uses
upstream Zimodem firmware authored by Bo Zimmerman (available on Github). With the
Arduino IDE you are free to install any firmware you see fit. The device is powered by a
MicroUSB or USB-C cable and requires 5V to operate. Connect the WiFiModem directly to
your DB25 serial port, or use a straight-through extension cable and/or DB9 adapter
and/or gender changer, if necessary. Supports common baud rates (300, 1200, 2400, 4800,
9600, 19200+).
