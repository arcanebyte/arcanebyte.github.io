The adb2lisa is a keyboard adapter that connects keyboards using the Apple Desktop Bus
(ADB) interface to an Apple Lisa or Macintosh XL system. With the adb2lisa keyboard
adapter, ADB-based keyboards behave like an original Lisa keyboard!

The adb2lisa comes fully assembled, tested, and ready for use.

## Compatibility

The adb2lisa hardware is compatible with all ADB-based keyboards using a mini DIN 4-pin
ADB connector, including keyboards provided with most early Macintosh computers and the
Apple IIgs. Third party keyboards used with Macintosh clones are also known to work.
