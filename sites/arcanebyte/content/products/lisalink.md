LisaLink goes in any of the Lisa's three expansion slots and connects it to a modern
network over wired Ethernet or WiFi.

On the Lisa Office System, it comes with its own TCP/IP stack and a Network Utility.
Support for MacWorks, with AppleTalk and MacTCP, is planned, along with a web browser that
handles https on the card.

## Status

The Rev A board is drawn and nothing has been built yet. The Office System software has
only run in the LisaEm emulator. There's no price yet.
