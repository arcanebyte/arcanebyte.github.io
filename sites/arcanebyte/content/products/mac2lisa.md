The mac2lisa is a keyboard adapter that connects Apple M0110 and M0110a keyboards used
with the Macintosh 128k, Macintosh 512k, and Macintosh Plus to an Apple Lisa or
Macintosh XL system. With the mac2lisa keyboard adapter, those vintage Mac keyboards
behave like an original Lisa keyboard!

The mac2lisa comes fully assembled, tested, and ready for use.

## Compatibility

The mac2lisa hardware is compatible with all original Apple M0110 and M0110a keyboards
provided with the Macintosh 128k, Macintosh 512k, and Macintosh Plus, including the
M0120 numeric keypad. Third party keyboards using the same interface may also work.
