Keyboard adapters

Use a USB, ADB, or original Macintosh keyboard on a Lisa or Macintosh XL.

  • adb2lisa

    Connects Apple Desktop Bus (ADB) keyboards to the Apple Lisa and Macintosh XL.

    Fits Apple Lisa, Macintosh XL

  • mac2lisa

    Connects Macintosh 128k/512k/Plus keyboards to the Apple Lisa and Macintosh XL.

    Fits Apple Lisa, Macintosh XL

  • usb2lisa

    Connects Universal Serial Bus (USB) keyboards to the Apple Lisa and Macintosh XL.

Modems

Put a machine with an RS232 serial port on WiFi, and dial a BBS by IP address.

  • WiFiModem

    ESP8266-based wireless modem for vintage computers with an RS232 serial port.

    Fits Any machine with an RS232 serial port

    Male or female connector, chosen at checkout.

    Order

  • WiFiModem32

    ESP32-based wireless modem for vintage computers, with SSH support.

    Fits Any machine with an RS232 serial port

    Male or female connector, chosen at checkout.

    Order
