Goodies for machines nobody supports.

Hand-built adapters, modems, and drive emulators for the Apple Lisa, Macintosh XL, and Apple ///.

For the Apple Lisa

Also the Macintosh XL. The hard drive emulator fits the Apple /// too.

  • adb2lisaConnects Apple Desktop Bus (ADB) keyboards to the Apple Lisa and Macintosh XL.
  • mac2lisaConnects Macintosh 128k/512k/Plus keyboards to the Apple Lisa and Macintosh XL.
  • usb2lisaConnects Universal Serial Bus (USB) keyboards to the Apple Lisa and Macintosh XL.
  • Apple Parallel Hard Drive EmulatorA ProFile hard drive emulator for the Apple Lisa and Apple ///, based on Cameo/Aphid.

In development

  • LisaBoostA faster 68000 and up to 32 MB of memory
  • Apple IIe CardA real 65C02 and the enhanced IIe's ROMs on a card so the Lisa can run Apple II software.
  • LisaColorA video card that gives MacWorks a 16-color desktop on a modern display.
  • LisaLinkAn Ethernet and WiFi card that puts the Lisa on a modern network.

For anything with an RS232 serial port

Any computer or terminal with a serial port.

  • WiFiModemESP8266-based wireless modem for vintage computers with an RS232 serial port.
  • WiFiModem32ESP32-based wireless modem for vintage computers, with SSH support.
