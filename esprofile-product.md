### Description

The **ESProfile Hard Drive Emulator** faithfully emulates the [Apple ProFile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apple_ProFile) series of hard disk drives introduced in 1981 for the Apple /// Computer and later the Apple Lisa and Macintosh XL. With little effort, one can install Lisa Office System, MacWorks, SOS, and other operating systems for the Apple Lisa, Macintosh XL, and Apple ///.

The ESProfile is the successor to the ArduinoFile by Alex McLeod-Anderson (AlexTheCat123). Information about the ESProfile and Selector (by Tom Stepleton) can be found at the following links:

- ESProfile: [https://github.com/alexthecat123/ESProFile](https://github.com/alexthecat123/ESProFile)
- Selector: [https://github.com/stepleton/cameo/tree/master/aphid/selector](https://github.com/stepleton/cameo/tree/master/aphid/selector)

The emulator is written for the ESP32 and works by presenting a disk image file as an Apple ProFile hard drive to the connected system. It supports 5MB and 10MB "ProFile" disk sizes, as well as ardbitrary sizes for operating systems that support it (i.e. MacWorks).

The **ESProfile** has been made available for sale by ArcaneByte with limited support. There are two variants of this product. Choose 'Internal' or 'External' at checkout.

- **External** connects to the built-in DB-25 parallel port of an Apple Lisa 1, Apple Lisa 2/5, or 2-port parallel card.

- **Internal** connects to the built-in 26-pin parallel interface of an Apple Lisa 2/10, and with a long 26-pin to DB-25 adapter can be used in a Lisa 2/5 or similar.

Both designs have been made available for [download](https://github.com/alexthecat123/ESProFile/tree/main/hw) in KiCad format should you choose to build one yourself.
